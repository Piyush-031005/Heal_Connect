Admin Panel Roles, Capabilities & Access Matrix

7. Role Hierarchy & Definitions

SUPERADMIN

The system owner. Has absolute, unrestricted access to the entire platform. Only a Superadmin can invite, edit, or delete other admins. They control API keys, system-wide financial configurations, and master payouts.

MODERATOR

The content & safety enforcer. Responsible for reviewing flagged calls/transcripts (Deepgram AI flags), verifying practitioner KYC documents, and issuing warnings/bans to users who violate platform policies.

SUPPORT

The customer service representative. Can view user profiles, session histories, and wallet ledgers to assist with support tickets. Can issue limited refunds but cannot ban users or approve official KYC documents.

VIEWER

The auditor / investor. Has strict read-only access. Can view analytics dashboards, revenue reports, and aggregate data, but cannot modify any records, interact with users, or view sensitive PII (Personally Identifiable Information).

Terminology Guide

  • No PII (Personally Identifiable Information): The Viewer role can see aggregate data (e.g., total active users) but cannot see private details like real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment data.
  • Escalation Only: The Support role can view AI-flagged conversations if a user complains, but they cannot make the final decision to ban an expert or dismiss the flag. They must click "Escalate" to forward the issue to a Moderator or Superadmin.
  • Up to limit: The Support role is authorized to issue immediate manual refunds to users, but only up to a system-defined cap (e.g., maximum $50 or ₹500 per transaction). Larger refunds require Superadmin approval to prevent fraud.
