ZenAuraa: Commission & Revenue Flow

A high-level overview of how money enters the platform, how the 15% commission is split, and how it is paid out.

1. The Flow of Money (with Stripe Fees)

User Recharges Wallet

User pays £100 via credit card.

Stripe Processing Fee

Stripe deducts ~2.9% + 30¢ (approx £3.20).

Company Bank Account

£96.80 is deposited into the actual company bank account. (But the user's app wallet shows £100).

Active Session Billing

User connects with an Expert charging £10/min. The system automatically deducts £10 per minute.

Platform Fee (15%)

£1.50 per minute is recorded as actual Company Revenue.

Expert Earnings (85%)

£8.50 per minute is added to the Expert's digital balance.

Payout Cycle (End of Month)

The company wires the 85% earnings to the Expert. The Expert pays the Stripe withdrawal fee (e.g., £2.00 flat fee or 0.25%). The Expert receives their 85% minus the fee.

2. Where is the Data Shown?

Admin Dashboard

Gross Volume: Shows the total amount spent by all users.

Actual Revenue: Shows the 15% platform cut collected across all sessions. This is the company's true profit.

Expert Dashboard

Total Earnings: Shows only their 85% cut. The expert never sees the 15% platform fee taken from their session.

3. Technical Implementation

The split occurs in real-time during the active session. Every 60 seconds, the billing engine (running in the backend) safely debits the user's wallet, instantly calculating the 15% platformFee and 85% expertEarnings. These values are permanently stored in the database for accurate payout reports, ensuring the platform's revenue figures are exact and fully audited.

4. True Net Profit (The Stripe Impact)

Because the 15% platform fee is calculated on the Gross Volume (the full £100 the user thinks they have), the company's 15% cut has to absorb the Stripe fees. Here is the true margin on a £100 spend:

  • Gross Platform Fee (15%): +£15.00
  • Minus Stripe Inbound Fee: -£3.20
  • True Net Profit: £11.80 (An effective margin of ~11.8%)

*Note: ZenAuraa's profit is completely protected from outbound payout fees. When the expert withdraws their money, the Stripe withdrawal/wire fee is deducted directly from the expert's earnings, not from ZenAuraa's margins.
